Hisbah Live on web and Android

A shop's debt book, on the phone.

Hisbah replaces the paper ledger in small grocery shops. Staff sell from an Android phone, each customer's debt stays under their name, and the owner runs stock and reports from any browser.

Sell by barcode or by weight
Scan the same item twice and the quantity goes up. Kilo items weigh to the gram.
Debt with a limit
A part payment leaves the rest as debt, with a due date and a cap staff can't pass.
Invoices stay on record
A mistake is cancelled with a reason. Stock comes back and the invoice stays in the log.
